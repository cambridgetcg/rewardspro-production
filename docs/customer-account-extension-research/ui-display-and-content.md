# Research Instructions: UI Display and Customer-Facing Content

## Objectives
- Understand how customer account extensions are embedded within the online store and how they inherit styling.
- Determine the content strategy for presenting customer data, rewards, or actions within the account dashboard.
- Identify customization levers (theme app extensions, settings, translations) that affect the display.

## Research Tasks
1. Examine Shopify's guidance on rendering within the customer account area, including layout constraints and theming.
2. Research how to manage typography, spacing, and components using Polaris or custom styling under Shopify's constraints.
3. Determine how the extension can respond to customer interactions (buttons, forms, navigation) and what event handlers are available.
4. Investigate localization and multi-language support for content displayed in the extension.
5. Explore strategies for surfacing contextual help, tooltips, or support links inside the account UI.
6. Document how the extension integrates with the online store's navigation and whether deep links are supported.

## Deliverables
- Wireframe sketches or references that illustrate desired layout and key interactions.
- Content inventory outlining what data points, CTAs, and messaging the extension should surface.
- Styling guidelines mapping Polaris components (or alternatives) to extension requirements.
