# Customer Account UI Extension Research Instructions

This collection of instruction sheets defines targeted research needed to close knowledge gaps before implementing a Shopify Customer Account UI extension. Each document focuses on a specific dimension of the implementation effort. Review all files, prioritize gaps that affect critical path tasks, and document findings in the shared research log.

## Instruction Files

- `best-practices.md` – Research Shopify and general extension best practices for customer account experiences.
- `cli-and-tooling.md` – Investigate the Shopify CLI workflow, supporting tooling, and automation strategies.
- `extension-architecture.md` – Understand extension architecture, APIs, and data contracts.
- `ui-display-and-content.md` – Clarify how the extension renders information in the customer account area.
- `data-and-integrations.md` – Explore data sources, backend integrations, and synchronization patterns.
- `testing-and-deployment.md` – Determine testing strategies, preview flows, and deployment processes.
- `security-and-compliance.md` – Identify security, privacy, and compliance considerations for customer data.

Record open questions that surface while following these instructions and iterate on the documents as new gaps emerge.
