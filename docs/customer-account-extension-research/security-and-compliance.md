# Research Instructions: Security and Compliance

## Objectives
- Ensure customer account data is handled securely within the extension and supporting systems.
- Identify regulatory requirements that impact data collection, storage, and display.
- Determine Shopify's security expectations for apps and extensions.

## Research Tasks
1. Review Shopify's security requirements for public apps and customer account extensions, including OAuth scopes and data handling policies.
2. Investigate authentication and authorization patterns when the extension calls backend services.
3. Determine how to protect sensitive customer information in transit and at rest (HTTPS, encryption, tokenization).
4. Identify compliance obligations (GDPR, CCPA, PCI if payments are surfaced) and their implications for UI design and data flows.
5. Research Shopify's audit, logging, and data retention guidelines relevant to customer accounts.
6. Document incident response expectations and communication protocols if a breach occurs.

## Deliverables
- Security checklist covering extension code, backend services, and Shopify configuration.
- Compliance matrix mapping regulations to required controls or documentation.
- Summary of outstanding security risks with recommended mitigations or follow-up actions.
