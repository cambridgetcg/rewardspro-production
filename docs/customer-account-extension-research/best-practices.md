# Research Instructions: Best Practices

## Objectives
- Identify Shopify's recommended patterns for Customer Account UI extensions and app embeds.
- Determine UX heuristics for presenting loyalty, rewards, or account data inside the customer portal.
- Capture accessibility and performance guidelines specific to Shopify's online store and account surfaces.

## Research Tasks
1. Review Shopify's official documentation on Customer Account UI extensions, focusing on guidelines and design considerations.
2. Study Polaris design system resources to understand UI conventions applicable to customer-facing components.
3. Gather case studies or example implementations from Shopify partners to benchmark best practices.
4. Investigate cross-device considerations (desktop, mobile web, mobile app) and how responsive design is handled within extensions.
5. Document recommended content hierarchy, interactions, and empty state handling for account dashboards.

## Deliverables
- Summary of top 10 best practices with supporting sources.
- Checklist mapping best practices to current project requirements.
- Noted gaps where Shopify's guidance is unclear or evolving.
